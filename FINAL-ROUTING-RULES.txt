FINAL ROUTING

HOME/CATEGORY PAGES:
Latest News + 6 Cards -> details.html#ID (no ?id=)

DETAILS PAGE:
Selected news opens in left Main Container with image, date, category, headline and full details.
A new middle-bottom Solo Ad is added below the full news.
Total Details ads: top + middle-top + middle-bottom + bottom.

DETAILS PAGE SIDEBAR + 6 CARDS:
Each clicked news is routed by its category to that category page with clean hash ID:
National -> national.html#ID
Politics -> politics.html#ID
Economy -> economy.html#ID
Sports -> sports.html#ID
Entertainment -> entertainment.html#ID
International -> international.html#ID
Technology -> technology.html#ID
The category page reads the hash and opens that ID as the full main news.
